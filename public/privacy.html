3. Who we share it with

We only share your information with the services we need to run the shop:

  • Stripe – payment processing and fraud prevention. Stripe also acts as an independent controller for some data, under its own privacy policy.
  • Postal and courier services (such as Royal Mail) – your name and delivery address, to deliver your order.
  • Email and messaging providers (Google for email, WhatsApp for messages) – if you contact us through them.
  • Our website hosting provider – which stores the site and its technical logs.
  • Our accountant, HMRC or other authorities – where the law requires it.

4. Transfers outside the UK

Some of these providers (for example Stripe, Google and WhatsApp) may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is protected by UK-approved safeguards such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement / Addendum.

5. How long we keep it

  • Order and payment records: 6 years from the end of the financial year they relate to, as required for UK company tax and accounting records.
  • Customer messages not linked to an order: up to 2 years, then deleted.
  • Website technical logs: kept only as long as our host retains them for security.

6. Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and does not track you. When you click “Buy now” you go to Stripe’s checkout, which uses its own essential cookies to process the payment securely and prevent fraud – see Stripe’s cookie policy for details.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, where we no longer need it or are not required to keep it;
  • object to or ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • ask for your information in a portable format.

To use any of these rights, email us. We will reply within one month. There is no charge in most cases.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
